## Why the complete 23220CA/W33 designation mattered

“23220 bearing” is not enough for an accurate industrial purchase. The suffixes describe construction details that can affect how the bearing fits the maintenance requirement. For this order, the controlled reference was 23220CA/W33.

The standard boundary dimensions associated with this designation are:

| Item | Catalog reference |
|---|---|
| Bore diameter | 100 mm |
| Outside diameter | 180 mm |
| Width | 60.3 mm |
| Bearing type | Double-row spherical roller bearing |
| Bore form | Cylindrical bore unless a K suffix is specified |
| CA construction | Machined brass cage design |
| W33 feature | Lubrication groove and holes in the outer ring |

Dimensions are useful cross-checks, but they do not replace the full manufacturer designation. A 23220 with a tapered-bore suffix, another cage design or a different internal-clearance suffix may not be the bearing requested by the maintenance team.

## Visible condition review before packing

The inspection photos show clean raceway and roller surfaces where visible, a complete cage and protective wrapping around the bearing. No visible transport damage is apparent in the photographed area.

The check should still be understood for what it is. A photo cannot establish internal geometry, dimensional tolerance or operating performance. For critical applications, a buyer may add measurement, preservation-condition review, document checking or manufacturer-channel verification to the agreed inspection scope.

Useful inspection points for a spherical roller bearing include:

- Complete ring marking and brand reference.
- Bore, outside diameter and width where dimensional checking is required.
- Correct cylindrical or tapered bore.
- Cage construction and visible completeness.
- Internal-clearance suffix, if specified.
- Rust, pitting, dents, contamination or damaged preservation.
- Condition of the individual box and outer shipping carton.
- Quantity count and model separation for mixed orders.

## Protective packing for the Argentina order

After the model and visible condition were recorded, the bearing boxes were wrapped together with cushioning material and protective film. This keeps the individual cartons grouped and reduces movement and surface damage during handling.

Bearings should remain in their preservation packaging until installation. For sea or consolidated freight, the final outer carton should protect against crushing and moisture, and the bearing package should not be placed beneath machinery or other loads that can deform the box.

## What buyers should send for a bearing quotation

To avoid an incomplete bearing comparison, overseas buyers should provide:

- Brand preference and whether alternatives may be quoted.
- Complete bearing number with every prefix and suffix.
- Required quantity for each model.
- Photos of the old bearing marking and box label.
- Bore type and internal-clearance requirement.
- Seals, shields, cage or lubrication features where relevant.
- Machine brand, model and operating position.
- Speed, load, temperature and environment for technical review.
- Document, inspection and packing requirements.
- Destination and required shipping schedule.

## FAQ

### What are the dimensions of a 23220CA/W33 bearing?
The standard catalog dimensions are 100 mm bore, 180 mm outside diameter and 60.3 mm width. The exact manufacturer designation and suffixes should still be checked.

### What does CA/W33 mean on a spherical roller bearing?
CA generally identifies a machined brass cage construction, while W33 identifies an outer-ring lubrication groove and holes. Manufacturer conventions should be confirmed for the exact brand.

### Can 23220CA/W33 replace every 23220 bearing?
No. Bore type, cage design, internal clearance, lubrication features and application requirements can differ. Compare the full bearing code and machine requirement.
