## Common applications in metal workshops

This type of belt sander for metal finishing is usually selected for practical workshop jobs rather than high-volume automated finishing. Typical applications include stainless steel polishing, deburring cut metal parts, edge grinding after sawing or laser cutting, removing small weld marks, cleaning sharp corners, and surface preparation before painting or assembly.

For stainless steel and metal parts, the sanding belt grade matters as much as the machine itself. A workshop may need abrasive options for rough grinding, intermediate finishing, and lighter polishing. If the machine will be used on stainless steel, carbon steel, aluminum, or mixed metal parts, describe the material and finish expectation before ordering spare belts.

A maintenance department may need a workshop grinding machine for deburring and edge finishing when repairing brackets, guards, shafts, welded frames, fixtures, and other small items that keep production equipment running.

## Key specifications buyers should confirm

When learning how to source industrial belt sander from China, the first step is to move from a general product name to a written technical confirmation. For this model, the most important points are belt size, voltage, frequency, power, speed, dimensions, net weight, and material application.

Voltage should not be treated as a small detail. This case uses an industrial belt grinder 380V 50Hz for workshop use. Buyers in countries with different electrical standards should confirm whether the machine can match local power conditions or needs adaptation. Motor labeling, plug type, cable length, emergency stop, and switch quality should also be checked.

Ask for practical photos or short videos showing the exact machine configuration. Look at the belt tracking, transparent guard, work rest, base stability, motor position, control box, and painted frame finish. These checks reduce the risk of receiving a machine that differs from the quotation photo.

## Buyer checklist before sourcing from China

Before confirming an order, use a checklist:

- Confirm model, belt size, voltage, frequency, rated power, belt speed, machine size, and net weight in writing.
- Ask whether the quoted machine is the same configuration as the photos and test video.
- Confirm whether spare sanding belts can be supplied together with the machine.
- Check the intended material: stainless steel, carbon steel, aluminum, or general metal parts.
- Confirm the packing method, wooden case size, gross weight, and whether export packing is suitable.
- Ask for the machine nameplate or motor plate photo before shipment.
- Clarify lead time, payment terms, warranty wording, and replacement part support.
- Compare quotations by specification, packing, and communication quality, not only unit price.

For a China industrial sourcing partner for small factory equipment, this kind of checklist is part of daily work. Small machines still need clear confirmation, because a low-value mistake can create downtime, rework, and extra freight cost.

## Inspection points before shipment

Pre-shipment inspection for a workshop belt sander does not need to be complicated, but it should be specific. The supplier should show that the belt runs smoothly, the motor starts normally, the emergency stop or switch works, the belt tracks correctly, the guard is installed, and the visible condition matches the order.

Useful inspection photos include front view, side view, motor plate, control switch, belt area, guard, base, packing preparation, and accessories. A short running video helps confirm noise, vibration, belt movement, and basic operation.

Packing should also be checked. A 115 kg machine needs stable support in the wooden case. Confirm whether the machine is fixed to the base, vulnerable parts are protected, and outer marks match the shipping documents.
