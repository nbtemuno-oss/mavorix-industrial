## What buyers should confirm on a spacer and screw RFQ

For custom steel spacers, include a drawing or sample, the material grade, critical diameters and lengths, bore or thread details, tolerance, finish and required quantity. If several spacer variants look alike, assign each one a distinct item number and state the bag quantity and carton label format.

For a countersunk screw, specify the complete size, how the length is measured, thread pitch and threaded length, head angle and dimensions, drive type, material and finish. A description such as “M5 x 90 countersunk screw” is a useful start, but it may not be enough to prevent a mismatch in assembly.

Also agree on packing before the order is released: pieces per bag, bags per carton, labels, carton count, pallet or crate requirements and destination marks. If goods from more than one supplier are involved, confirm whether the shipment should be consolidated and how each item will remain traceable after repacking.
