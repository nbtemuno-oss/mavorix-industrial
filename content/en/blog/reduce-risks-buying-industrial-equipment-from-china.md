## Define the equipment requirement before supplier search

Industrial equipment purchasing should begin with capacity, application, materials, power requirements, layout, control needs, spare parts, and destination market requirements.

## Do not rely only on product photos

Photos can show appearance, but they rarely prove suitability. Ask for technical parameters, videos, drawings, component lists, testing information, and references to similar equipment types without relying on invented case claims.

## Compare supplier capability

Evaluate whether the supplier understands your application, answers technical questions clearly, and can provide realistic delivery and support information.

## Confirm quality checkpoints

Define inspection and testing points before production. For machinery, this may include appearance, configuration, running test, packing, spare parts list, voltage, and documentation.

## Control export communication

Confirm packing dimensions, gross weight, loading plan, documents, shipping term, and delivery timing. Export coordination problems can create delays even when the product is ready.

### FAQ: What is the biggest risk when buying industrial equipment from China?
The biggest risk is often unclear specification and supplier capability, not just price.

### FAQ: Should I inspect machinery before shipment?
Yes, pre-shipment checks and running tests can reduce problems after export.

### FAQ: Can a sourcing partner manage all risk?
No partner can remove all risk, but structured supplier screening, technical communication, and quality coordination can reduce risk significantly.

### FAQ: What documents should I check?
Check quotation, proforma invoice, packing list, commercial invoice, technical documents, photos, and shipping information.
