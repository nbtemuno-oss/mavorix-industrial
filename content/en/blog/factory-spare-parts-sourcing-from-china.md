## Factory spare parts sourcing starts with identification

Factory spare parts sourcing from China often begins with an urgent problem: a machine is stopped, a production line is unstable, or a maintenance team needs backup parts before the next shutdown. The buyer may have a broken part on the table, a faded label, or a machine manual with incomplete information.

In this situation, the first task is not price negotiation. The first task is correct identification. A wrong spare part can waste more time than a higher price. This is especially true for hydraulic components, electrical controls, pneumatic parts, bearings, seals, tooling, and packaging machine spare parts.

## Prepare clear photos and technical clues

For each spare part, prepare a practical information package:

- Photos of the complete part
- Close-up photos of nameplates, labels, ports, connectors, seals, threads, and mounting holes
- Product dimensions and installation dimensions
- Machine brand and model
- Application position on the machine
- Working conditions such as voltage, pressure, speed, temperature, or material contact
- Required quantity and urgency
- Whether exact original brand or compatible replacement is acceptable

When a part number is missing, photos and dimensions become even more important. See also the guide on how to [identify industrial spare parts without a part number](/en/blog/identify-industrial-spare-parts-without-part-number/).

## Separate critical parts from common parts

Not all factory spare parts carry the same risk. Before asking for quotations, classify items by risk:

- Critical parts that can stop production
- Safety-related electrical or mechanical parts
- Standard consumables and wear parts
- Non-critical workshop parts
- Items used for inventory backup

Critical parts need stricter confirmation. Buyers may require original brand, exact model code, test reports, supplier documents, or photo confirmation before shipment. Common consumables may only need dimensional confirmation, material confirmation, and packing checks.

## Compare original parts, compatible replacements, and local alternatives

Chinese suppliers may quote different types of spare parts:

- Original imported brand
- Authorized distributor stock
- Domestic Chinese equivalent
- Compatible replacement
- Custom-made part based on sample or drawing

Each option has a different risk profile. Original brand parts may be safer for critical equipment, but price and lead time can be higher. Compatible replacements may be practical for non-critical applications if the technical match is clear. Custom-made parts may work for mechanical parts, but drawings, material, heat treatment, tolerance, and surface finish must be confirmed.

The buyer should ask suppliers to label each quotation clearly, so original parts and replacements are not mixed together.

## Use supplier checks for technical and commercial risk

Factory spare parts sourcing can involve small suppliers, stock dealers, workshops, distributors, and trading companies. Some are very useful, but buyers should check whether the supplier is suitable for the specific item.

Useful checks include:

- Business scope and supplier category
- Product category experience
- Real product photos
- Stock confirmation
- Brand or authorization documents when needed
- Delivery reliability
- Packing capability
- Communication quality

For unfamiliar suppliers, [supplier verification](/en/services/supplier-verification/) can reduce uncertainty before payment.

## Confirm the part before export

Before goods leave China, ask for confirmation photos. For spare parts, useful photo checks include:

- Model code and nameplate
- Quantity
- Packaging label
- Connector, mounting face, and visible key details
- Accessories included
- Carton or wooden-case packing

For some products, functional testing may not be possible without the buyer's machine. In that case, photo checking, document review, and supplier confirmation become more important.

## Keep repeat order records

Factories often buy the same spare parts again. A simple record can save time later:

- Buyer item code
- Original request photos
- Final purchased model
- Supplier name
- Price and lead time
- Inspection photos
- Packing method
- Shipment date
- Comments from the maintenance team after use

This record also helps compare future quotations and avoid changing suppliers without reason.

### FAQ: What should I send when sourcing factory spare parts from China?
Send photos, nameplates, model codes, dimensions, machine information, quantity, urgency, and whether replacements are acceptable.

### FAQ: Can Chinese suppliers identify parts from photos?
Sometimes. Clear photos from several angles, close-ups of labels, and dimensions improve the chance of accurate identification.

### FAQ: Are compatible spare parts safe to use?
Compatible parts may be suitable for some applications, but critical parts should be checked carefully against the machine requirements.
