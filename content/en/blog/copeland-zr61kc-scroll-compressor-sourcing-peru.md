## R22 compatibility and destination checks

The ZR61KC-TFD model reference is associated with R22 air-conditioning service. Refrigerant regulations and service availability vary by country and change over time. Before importing an R22 compressor, the buyer should confirm current Peruvian requirements and whether the existing system can legally and practically be maintained.

Changing refrigerant is not a label substitution. It may affect lubricant, expansion device, seals, controls, capacity and operating envelope. Any conversion should be engineered and commissioned by qualified HVAC personnel using approved technical guidance.

For international transport, a compressor should remain upright and restrained at the mounting base. Copper connections and the terminal box should not carry transport loads. The final packing choice should reflect compressor weight, route, consolidation plan and unloading method. A wooden outer case may be added when the shipment or surrounding cargo requires greater crush protection.

Yes. It is a hermetic Copeland Scroll compressor used in air-conditioning applications.

Copeland's published ZR61KC-TFD data lists a 5 HP nominal motor. Cooling capacity depends on the rating and site operating conditions.

### What power supply is associated with ZR61KC-TFD?
