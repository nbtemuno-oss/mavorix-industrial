## Understanding the TR-T 20×20 Specification

TR-T was the product type or shape code used in the purchasing information for this order. The 20×20 marking was the nominal size used in the same purchasing reference.

The product photo shows gray ceramic abrasive chips with visible angled faces and a broadly triangular or wedge-like profile. That visual confirmation is useful, but it should not be stretched beyond what the source information supports. The photo does not confirm the ceramic composition, alumina percentage, hardness, abrasive grade, cut rate, wear rate, density or exact dimensional tolerances.

Different manufacturers may use slightly different internal codes for ceramic media shapes. Buyers should therefore compare the physical sample, dimensional drawing or supplier specification rather than relying only on a short code. If a buyer is replacing an existing mass finishing media, a clear photo of the old media and a sample are often more reliable than a general description such as "ceramic polishing stone."

## Why Shape and Size Need to Be Confirmed

Ceramic deburring media is selected according to the workpiece, the finishing target and the machine or process used by the buyer. A shape that works well on open surfaces may not reach narrow recesses. Smaller media may reach finer details but may also create separation or lodging concerns. Larger media may be easier to separate but may not reach small features.

Before buying bulk ceramic finishing media, the buyer should confirm the practical application questions:

<ul>
  <li>What material are the workpieces?</li>
  <li>What burr, edge or surface defect must be removed?</li>
  <li>Is the required result deburring, edge rounding, cleaning or polishing?</li>
  <li>Can the media reach grooves, corners and recessed surfaces?</li>
  <li>Is there a risk of media lodging in holes or slots?</li>
  <li>What finishing machine or mass-finishing process will be used?</li>
  <li>Is a sample test required before mass production?</li>
</ul>

Application suitability depends on the workpiece and finishing target. A TR-T 20x20 ceramic abrasive media request can be clear for purchasing, but the final processing result should still be confirmed through sample testing when the workpiece or finishing target is new.

## Checks Before a 10-Ton Bulk Order

For a large quantity of bulk ceramic finishing media, the practical checks should focus on the specification, visible product consistency and packing condition.

The China-side purchasing check can include:

<ul>
  <li>product shape compared with the approved sample or photo</li>
  <li>nominal size confirmation against the purchasing specification</li>
  <li>visible color and surface consistency</li>
  <li>broken-media proportion visible during inspection</li>
  <li>bag condition and whether the bags are suitable for handling</li>
  <li>bag quantity based on supplier packing records</li>
  <li>packing-mark information when available</li>
  <li>pallet arrangement before shipment preparation</li>
  <li>protection against moisture and handling damage</li>
  <li>batch identification when available</li>
  <li>supplier confirmation of the selected abrasive grade</li>
</ul>

These are purchasing and packing checks. They do not replace laboratory composition analysis, hardness testing, wear-rate testing, cutting-rate testing or full dimensional inspection of every individual piece. For a new workpiece or finishing target, visual inspection and packing checks should be combined with sample processing before a large repeat order.

The ceramic media was packed in woven bags and arranged on wooden pallets. The visible palletized loads were secured with plastic wrapping, vertical straps and wooden top protection.

This kind of packing review is important because ceramic abrasive chips are heavy industrial consumables. Even when the product itself is simple, poor bag condition, unstable stacking or weak pallet protection can create handling problems before export shipment.

## What Buyers Should Send When Requesting Ceramic Tumbling Media

The fastest ceramic abrasive media inquiry is usually the one with photos, dimensions and process context. A photo of the existing media and finished workpiece is often more useful than a general request such as "ceramic polishing stone."

## FAQ

### What is ceramic tumbling media used for?
Ceramic tumbling media is commonly used in mass finishing for deburring, edge smoothing, cleaning and surface finishing. The final result depends on the selected media grade, workpiece material, part geometry, equipment and finishing target.

### What does TR-T 20×20 mean?
TR-T 20×20 was the product type and nominal size code used in this purchasing case. Different suppliers may use different internal codes, so buyers should confirm the code together with a sample, product photo, drawing or specification sheet.

### How do I choose the right ceramic media shape and size?
Consider the workpiece material, burr position, holes, slots, recessed areas, required final surface and finishing-machine type. Sample testing is advisable when the media shape or workpiece is new.

### Can the same ceramic media be used for every metal part?
No. Different metals, burr conditions and surface requirements may need different abrasive grades, shapes and sizes. Application suitability depends on the workpiece and finishing target.

### Should a sample test be completed before ordering 10 tons?
For a new workpiece or process, sample testing is advisable before committing to a large bulk order. Repeat orders based on an already approved sample may follow the confirmed specification.
