## How to choose a POF shrink film supplier in China

POF, or polyolefin shrink film, is widely used for retail multipacks, printed products, household goods, food-contact applications where the selected grade is suitable, and general protective overwrapping. Overseas buyers may search for <strong>POF shrink film rolls</strong>, <strong>polyolefin shrink wrap film</strong>, <strong>center-folded POF film</strong> or <strong>single-wound POF film</strong>. These terms are related, but they do not always describe the same roll format.

When comparing POF shrink film manufacturers or suppliers, start with the product and shrink-wrapping line. The correct film is determined by the pack dimensions, sealing equipment and shrink tunnel rather than width or price per kilogram alone. A supplier also needs to confirm whether mixed specifications can run within one order and whether the minimum quantity applies per size, per thickness or to the combined purchase.

Before comparing POF shrink film suppliers, a buyer should confirm:

- Flat or folded film format and the method used to state width.
- Film thickness and acceptable tolerance.
- Roll length, net film weight and gross packed weight.
- Core inside diameter, core length and roll outside diameter.
- Sealing method, shrink tunnel type and operating-temperature window.
- Required shrink behavior in the machine and transverse directions.
- Clarity, gloss, slip and perforation requirements where relevant.
- Food-contact, recycled-content or destination documentation where applicable.
- Carton marking, pallet size, maximum pallet height and unloading limits.

For a repeat order, the safest reference is an approved roll or a complete specification sheet tied to the customer’s actual packing line. Film that looks similar on a loose roll can behave differently during sealing and shrinking. For a new supplier, the buyer can request a trial roll and agree how width, thickness, roll length, sealing and shrink results will be checked before a wholesale POF film order proceeds.

## Individual roll protection inside the carton

The packing sequence for this order used a dedicated carton for each roll. A molded plastic locator was positioned at the bottom before the wrapped roll was lowered into the box.

A second locator and internal board were fitted above the roll before the carton was closed. Together, the upper and lower supports help locate the core and limit direct pressure on the film edges.

This type of packing is useful for long export routes because film rolls can be damaged by edge impact, core displacement, dust or moisture even when the outer carton still appears intact. The exact locator design should match the core and roll diameter rather than being treated as a universal insert.

## Palletizing the mixed POF film order

After individual carton packing, the boxes were stacked on wooden pallets and wrapped as complete pallet loads. The finished pallets were tall, rectangular units that could be moved by forklift without handling cartons one by one.

For a mixed-width order, pallet labels should identify the specification and quantity inside. This reduces sorting work at destination and makes it easier to reconcile delivered pallets against the packing list. Pallet footprint, stack height and gross weight should also be agreed before production so the load plan does not depend on last-minute adjustments.

Dunnage bags are one part of a loading plan, not a substitute for suitable pallets and stable stacking. Bag size, inflation pressure, void width and contact surfaces need to suit the cargo. The loading team should also check door clearance, floor loading, pallet condition and how the receiver will unload the container.

## FAQ

### What information is needed to source POF shrink film rolls from China?
Provide the film format, width, thickness, roll length or net weight, core size, roll diameter limit, sealing and shrink equipment, required performance, quantity, packing and destination. A sample or approved technical sheet is useful for repeat orders.

### Is center-folded POF shrink film the same as single-wound POF film?
No. Center-folded film is folded along its length and has two layers when unwound, while single-wound film is supplied as one flat web. Buyers should state the format and width convention clearly because the same width figure can describe a different usable film span.

### What is the MOQ for wholesale POF shrink film rolls?
MOQ depends on film grade, thickness, width, roll format, production schedule and packing. For a mixed container, ask whether the minimum applies to each specification or whether several widths can be combined under one production order.
