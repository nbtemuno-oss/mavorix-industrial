## Check whether the supplier matches the product

A supplier should be evaluated against the exact industrial product you need. A company that sells many unrelated categories may not be the right source for technical machinery, OEM parts, or MRO items.

## Review business and communication signals

Supplier verification includes business profile review, website consistency, product focus, export communication, response quality, quotation clarity, and whether the team can answer technical questions.

## Ask technical questions

Good questions reveal capability. Ask about materials, tolerances, standards, production process, testing method, packing, spare parts, and lead time. Weak or copied answers are risk signals.

## Confirm documents and order details

Before payment, confirm proforma invoice details, company name, bank account consistency, product specification, quantity, packing, delivery term, and inspection plan.

## Use inspection and staged orders

For higher-risk orders, staged payment, samples, trial orders, and pre-shipment inspection can reduce exposure. The inspection checklist should match the product, not a generic template.

### FAQ: Can I verify a Chinese supplier online only?
Online checks help, but industrial orders often need deeper review of product capability, communication quality, quotation details, and production evidence.

### FAQ: What are warning signs before payment?
Warning signs include unclear company identity, inconsistent bank details, vague technical answers, unrealistic lead times, and pressure for fast payment.

### FAQ: Is a business license enough?
A business license is useful but not enough. Buyers should also check product capability, communication, order details, and quality control arrangements.

For industrial goods, pre-shipment inspection is often useful because defects or packing issues are harder to solve after export.
