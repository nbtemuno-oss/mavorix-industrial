## Why MRO sourcing is different

MRO sourcing often involves small quantities, mixed categories, urgent timelines, and parts that are difficult to identify. A normal product search is rarely enough for factory maintenance needs.

## Build a clear MRO list

Group items by mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, consumable, tool, and replacement part categories. Add photos, nameplates, part numbers, dimensions, voltage, material, and application notes where possible.

## Identify equivalent products carefully

Many MRO items have alternatives, but compatibility matters. Confirm dimensions, model numbers, installation method, working conditions, and supplier warranty language before ordering.

## Consolidate when practical

When many small items come from different suppliers, consolidation can reduce communication problems and improve shipment control. It also helps buyers track packing and documents more clearly.

## Plan repeat supply

For factory maintenance parts, repeat supply can matter more than one-time price. Keep supplier records, item photos, part numbers, and inspection notes for future orders.

### FAQ: What does MRO mean?
MRO means maintenance, repair, and operation supplies used to keep factories and industrial equipment running.

### FAQ: Can China supply mixed MRO items?
Yes, but buyers need careful item identification, supplier matching, and shipment coordination.

### FAQ: What information is needed for spare parts?
Photos, model numbers, nameplates, dimensions, machine brand, application, and quantity are useful.

### FAQ: Can MRO orders be consolidated?
Yes. Multi-supplier MRO orders can often be consolidated for better shipment coordination.
