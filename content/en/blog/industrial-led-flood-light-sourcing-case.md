## What the Customer Needed

The customer needed a batch of robust flood lights for an industrial or commercial lighting application. The exact project location, order quantity and technical specification are not disclosed publicly.

During sourcing, the important task was to make sure the supplier understood the complete approved configuration rather than quoting only by product appearance. Two high power LED flood light products may look similar from the outside but differ in LED source, driver configuration, reflector design, heat dissipation, wiring, protection level and export packing.

For this type of industrial LED flood light sourcing work, the purchasing discussion should cover the housing design, mounting bracket, LED source, driver specification, reflector configuration, input voltage, wattage and lumen target, color temperature, beam requirement, environmental protection, cable and connector, and packing method. ## Heat Dissipation and Housing Checks

Heat dissipation is a major purchasing concern for commercial LED flood light products because thermal design affects the operating condition of the LED source and driver. The available photographs can support visible assembly review, but they do not replace thermal testing or a verified temperature-rise report.

Practical visual checks can include heat-sink fin condition, fin damage or deformation, housing coating consistency, screw and fastener installation, bracket alignment, contact between the LED module and heat-dissipation structure, driver placement if visible, and whether the fixture follows the approved ventilation or sealed construction design.

None of those checks confirms junction temperature, housing temperature, ambient operating limit or long-term life. Those values should be confirmed from supplier documentation, project test reports or the approved specification before ordering.

## Reflector and Optical Assembly

The reflector photos show a chamber with reflective surfaces and multiple LED openings. For an outdoor LED flood light or industrial floodlight supplier comparison, this part of the fixture should not be ignored. The optical assembly influences how light leaves the housing, even when the electrical specification appears similar.

Visible checks can include reflector surface condition, dents or deformation, mounting position, LED opening alignment, reflector cleanliness, internal foreign objects, edge condition and the relationship between the reflector and LED source.

Buyers should also confirm the required beam angle, lighting distribution, mounting height, target illuminated area, glare requirement and photometric files when they are required for the project. The available photographs do not confirm a specific beam angle or completed IES testing.

## Assembly and Power-On Checks

Supplier-side production photos can help the buyer follow batch assembly progress before shipment. In this case, the available photos showed multiple housings in the production area, visible wiring, bracket and clip installation, and a basic power-on lighting check in a dark environment.

These photographs can support checks for obvious housing damage, missing brackets or clips, wiring presence, LED sources turning on, visible color consistency, obvious dark or failed LED sources and preparation for packing.

A basic power-on check is not the same as a full photometric test, waterproof test, surge test, long-duration aging test, electrical safety certification or thermal performance test. Those items require supplier documentation or separate test records.

## What Buyers Should Confirm Before Ordering LED Flood Lights

A request such as "200W flood light" is usually not enough for an accurate quotation because two fixtures with the same wattage may differ in lumen output, optics, driver, protection rating and installation design.

Before requesting an LED flood light quality inspection or quotation, buyers should prepare:

<ul>
  <li>application and installation area</li>
  <li>required wattage</li>
  <li>required lumen output</li>
  <li>input voltage and frequency</li>
  <li>beam angle or lighting distribution</li>
  <li>mounting height</li>
  <li>color temperature</li>
  <li>CRI requirement</li>
  <li>IP and IK rating</li>
  <li>LED manufacturer or approved equivalent</li>
  <li>driver brand and specification</li>
  <li>surge protection requirement</li>
  <li>dimming or control method</li>
  <li>cable length and connector</li>
  <li>mounting-bracket dimensions</li>
  <li>certification requirement</li>
  <li>quantity</li>
  <li>packing method</li>
  <li>destination market</li>
</ul>

This buyer specification checklist helps suppliers quote the correct product and helps the buyer compare more than price. It is especially important when sourcing from an LED flood light manufacturer China market where many similar-looking housings may be offered with different internal configurations.

## FAQ

### What information is needed for an industrial LED flood light quotation?
Send the application, required wattage, lumen target, input voltage and frequency, beam angle, mounting height, color temperature, IP rating, driver requirement and quantity. Photos, drawings or a previous approved sample also help suppliers quote more accurately.

### Is wattage enough to compare two LED flood lights?
No. Wattage alone is not enough. Buyers should also compare lumen output, efficiency, optical distribution, driver specification, heat dissipation, protection rating, mounting design and required certification.

### Why is heat dissipation important for a high-power LED flood light?
Thermal management affects the operating condition of the LED source and driver. Specific performance should be confirmed through thermal testing, manufacturer documentation or approved project test reports rather than from appearance alone.

### What IP rating should be specified for an outdoor LED flood light?
The IP rating should be selected according to rain exposure, dust, cleaning method and installation environment. It should be checked against the buyer's project requirement and supported by supplier documentation.

### What should be checked during LED flood light production?
Useful checks include the approved BOM, housing appearance, heat sink, reflector, mounting bracket, wiring, basic power-on function, visible LED consistency and packing preparation.
